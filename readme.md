# Atelier — Commercial Space Planner 🏗️✨

Atelier is an interactive, browser-based **3D & 2D spatial layout tool** designed to help architects, interior designers, and commercial operators plan out their environments in seconds. Featuring fully realized digital twins and templates, users can immediately configure modern **offices, cafes, boutiques, and restaurants** while monitoring compliance and live budgeting.

## 🔥 Key Features

### 🏢 1. 3D Perspective & Floor Plan Toggle
Seamlessly switch between a top-down structural 2D technical layout sheet and an immersive, real-time 3D perspective to preview material finishes, depth, and spatial flow instantly.

### 📐 2. Smart Furniture Library & Click-to-Place
Equipped with a comprehensive commercial-grade furniture catalog. Click any object in the assets drawer to dynamically place it onto the workspace canvas, drag it to rotate, and adjust proximity.

### 📈 3. Live Capacity & Density Analytics
* **Real-time Seat Tracker:** Dynamically tallies seats placed versus total venue cap (e.g., *0/40 seats*).
* **Density Metric Engine:** Measures precise occupancy percentages and square meters/feet allocated per seat to prevent over-crowding.

### 🦺 4. Automated Fire Egress Compliance 
Stay up to code during the design process. The built-in compliance engine flags door clearances (requires minimum 1.2 meters) and verifies structural density guidelines based on assembly use constraints.

### 🎨 5. Instant Brand Recolor Engine
Change the aesthetic profile of your entire spatial design with one click. Input a single hex brand color (e.g., `#C75D3F`) to instantly recolor accent chairs, counters, pendants, branding strips, and shelving globally.

### 💰 6. Live Costing & PDF Quoting Export
* Generates live pricing sub-totals for every piece of furniture dropped onto the canvas.
* Accounts for commercial logistics, including Delivery & Installation (12%) and Contingency buffers (8%).
* One-click **Export PDF Document** to generate official Building Layout Sheets and full structural cost breakdowns.
